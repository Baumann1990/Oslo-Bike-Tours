Epic Marka Endurance

This is Nordmarka as few visitors — and even many Osloites — ever get to see it. A full-day push into the deep forest, far beyond the day-tripper trails, through a landscape of remote lakes and ancient logging roads that haven't changed in a century.

Your guide picks you up at your hotel and we make our way out to Sognsvann before heading north into sustained climbing — the kind that builds steadily through the trees and rewards you not with a sudden view but with the quiet satisfaction of distance covered and height gained. The route threads between lakes — Store Sandungen, Fyllingen, Hakkloa — on gravel roads that shift between fast and open and slow and rocky, where loose stones and rooted surfaces ask for a little patience and good line choice.

Somewhere in the middle of it all, you stop — the exact spot decided by you and your guide on the day. The return route takes a different line home, more climbing, more rocky stretches, more forest. By the end your legs will know about it. So will your face — from smiling.

Highlights

  • Long, sustained climbs deep into the Nordmarka forest
  • Hakkloa and Sandungen — off-the-beaten-track lakes

FAQ

Common questions

How physically demanding is the Epic Marka Endurance tour?

It is our most demanding tour by a significant margin — 85 km of gravel with approximately 1,500 metres of elevation gain over six hours. Good cycling fitness is genuinely required. This is a full-day ride into remote terrain, not a leisure outing. If you are unsure whether it suits your fitness level, email us and we can talk it through.

What food and drink is provided on the Epic Marka Endurance tour?

A proper break is taken during the ride — the exact spot is decided by the guide and guests on the day, depending on conditions and pace. We recommend bringing snacks and at least 1.5 litres of water from the start. There are no shops or cafés in the deep forest sections of the route.

What should I bring for the Epic Marka Endurance full-day ride?

Padded shorts are strongly recommended for an 85 km ride. Bring extra layers, a buff or hat, cycling gloves, and sunscreen for clear days. If there is a risk of rain in the forecast, pack waterproofs — conditions in Nordmarka can differ from the city. Your guide will have a basic first aid kit and puncture repair supplies.

Is the Epic Marka Endurance tour available on an e-bike?

No. The route is mostly well-maintained gravel with moderate climbs, but at 85 km it is simply too long for current e-bike range — there is nowhere to charge in the forest. We offer e-bike rentals on our shorter city and fjord tours if that suits better.

Can a group of mixed abilities do the Epic Marka Endurance tour together?

Yes, in most cases. The terrain is predominantly gravel with moderate climbs, so significant technical ability is not required. The main consideration is stamina — it is a long day, and everyone in the group should be comfortable riding for six hours. Stronger riders may find the climbs easier, but no one will be left struggling on technical ground.
