Private Oslo Bike Tours
With Hotel Pickup

Most bike tour operators require you to find a central meeting point — a square, a car park, a bike shop on the other side of town. On an unfamiliar city, that means map-checking, navigating public transport, and arriving already slightly frazzled before the tour has even started.

We do it differently. Your guide comes to you. Bikes in hand, at your door, at the agreed time. You step outside and the tour begins.

We pick up anywhere in Oslo. Whether you are staying at a grand hotel on Karl Johans gate, a boutique guesthouse in Grünerløkka, a rental apartment in Frogner or an Airbnb in Majorstuen — just share your address when you enquire and we will be there.

What's included

  • Direct pickup from your hotel, Airbnb or apartment
  • Bikes for the whole group — gravel or e-bike on request
  • Helmets included
  • Private guide for your group only
  • Flexible start time — you choose when we meet
  • No hidden fees or transfer surcharges

Airbnb and apartment pickup

A growing number of our guests stay in Airbnbs, serviced apartments or private rentals rather than traditional hotels. That works perfectly for us. Just include your full address and any relevant entry instructions in the booking form and your guide will find you without any fuss.

We always meet at street level outside your building — just share your address and your guide will be waiting outside at the agreed time.

FAQ

Common questions

Do Oslo Bike Tours include hotel pickup?

Yes — hotel pickup is included with every private tour we offer. Your guide will come directly to your hotel lobby at the agreed time. There is no meeting point to find, no bus to catch, and no extra charge for the pickup.

Can you pick up from an Airbnb or apartment in Oslo?

Absolutely. We pick up from Airbnbs, serviced apartments, private addresses and any other accommodation in Oslo. Just share your address when you book and your guide will be waiting outside at the agreed time.

Is there an extra charge for hotel or apartment pickup?

No. Pickup from your hotel, Airbnb or apartment anywhere in Oslo is included in the tour price. There are no hidden transfer fees or surcharges — what you see on the tour page is what you pay.

Which hotels in Oslo do you pick up from?

We pick up from all hotels in Oslo. Well-known properties where we regularly collect guests include The Thief and Amerikalinjen (Aker Brygge and Tjuvholmen), Grand Hotel and Hotel Continental (Karl Johans gate), Clarion Hotel The Hub (Sentrum), Sommerro (Frogner), and PS:Hotel and Citybox (Grünerløkka) — but this is nowhere near an exhaustive list. If your hotel is in Oslo, we will be there.

What time will my guide pick me up?

You choose the start time when you book. There are no fixed departure slots — tours run entirely by appointment so your guide arrives at whatever time suits your plans. Morning departures offer quieter streets; evening tours in summer benefit from Oslo's famously long golden hours.

Do I need to bring my own bike?

No. Bikes are included in the tour price. Your guide arrives at your door with bikes for the whole group. Gravel bikes and e-bikes are available on request — just mention your preference when you enquire and we will make sure the right bikes are ready.
