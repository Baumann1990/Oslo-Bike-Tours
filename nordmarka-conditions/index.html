How we rate a day

Each day is scored for the daylight hours you would be out in: from 10:00, when bikes can be collected in season, until sunset. In the riding season the verdict is about riding. In winter, when we are closed, the same scale simply describes the weather.

  • Good riding (shown as Dry in winter): dry, or close to it, with no strong wind.
  • Showers: some rain, roughly half a millimetre to three over the day. A shell jacket is enough.
  • Wet (shown as Rain in winter): steady or heavy rain. The gravel drains well and stays firm, so it is rideable, just wetter. Allow more braking distance.
  • Windy: dry but gusty. The forest shelters most of the riding; open lakesides and ridges feel it.
  • Snow: only when the forecast is for real snow, not cold rain.

Snow depth

Snow depth comes from seNorge, the snow model run by NVE and MET Norway, for two points: Sognsvann at the forest edge, around 180 metres, and Kikutstua deep in the Marka, around 350 metres. Each figure is the average for a square kilometre of forest, so a sheltered hollow can hold more and a sunny road less. The model gives today's depth and a forecast for the next nine days.

The two points rarely agree. The forest edge loses its snow first in spring and gets it last in autumn, while the high forest around Kikutstua can stay white for weeks after Sognsvann is clear. When both read zero, the gravel is back.

Why the forest, not the city

The forecast is for the Nordmarka itself, at around 280 metres, not for central Oslo. Up there it is usually a few degrees colder than the city, and on cold, wet days the difference between rain in town and something wintrier in the trees matters. If you are deciding whether to ride, this is the forecast that counts.

Common questions

Can you ride in the Nordmarka when it rains?

Yes. The forest roads are well-drained gravel that stays firm in rain, so a wet day is rideable, just wetter. Bring a shell jacket and allow more braking distance.

When does the gravel riding season in the Nordmarka end?

Usually when the first lasting snow arrives, often in November. Until then autumn is one of the best times to ride: quiet roads, autumn colours and firm gravel.

How late can I start Ring 4?

Ring 4 takes about five hours with stops. Start no later than five hours before sunset. In the riding season, the start-time table on this page shows the exact time for today and the next days.

How much snow is there in the Nordmarka?

It changes daily, and this page shows today's depth for two points: Sognsvann at the forest edge, around 180 metres, and Kikutstua deep in the Marka, around 350 metres, with the seNorge model's forecast for the next nine days. The deeper forest usually holds snow for weeks longer than the edge, both in late autumn and in spring.

Is the Nordmarka colder than central Oslo?

Usually a few degrees colder. The forecast on this page is for the forest itself at around 280 metres, near Ullevålseter, not for the city.
